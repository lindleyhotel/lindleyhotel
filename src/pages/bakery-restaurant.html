The bakery

Bakery & take-away

Our bakery counter sits at the end of the veranda, under the sign that says it all. Farm-style bread, golden rolletjies, savoury pies, homemade carrot cake, chocolate cake and fudge, plus take-away food for the road. Fresh bread and hot pies are ready every weekday morning.

The restaurant

Homestyle meals

Hearty country cooking in our sandstone dining room: farm-style breakfasts, rump steaks, handmade beef and prego burgers, the classic Dagwood, bobotie with yellow rice, lamb curry, toasted sandwiches, wraps and the cake of the day. Booking is recommended on weekends.
