Welcome

An old hotel that still keeps the kettle on

The Lindley Hotel was built in 1895, when Lindley was still a young town on the banks of the Vals River. Today it has been lovingly restored, keeping the character of the original building and adding the comforts travellers expect.

Couples stop over on long drives, families gather for celebrations, and locals come for fresh bread in the morning. Whatever brings you here, you're welcome to stay.
