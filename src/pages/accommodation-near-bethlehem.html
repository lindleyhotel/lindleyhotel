A peaceful base for the Bethlehem area

Bethlehem is the busy hub of the eastern Free State and the gateway to Clarens and the mountains. If you'd rather end the day somewhere quieter, the Lindley Hotel is a short drive up the R76: a restored 1895 hotel with lace-trimmed verandas, sandstone walls and a bakery that opens early.

It works well for guests visiting family in the region, travelling for farm or business work, or attending a wedding or function nearby, as well as for anyone who simply likes small-town hospitality.

  • Free, secure parking on the property
  • Luxury, double, twin and triple rooms, most en-suite
