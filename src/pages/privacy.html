Privacy notice

How we handle your personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What this website collects

This website does not have contact forms, user accounts or advertising trackers. It does not ask you for personal information.

When you contact us

If you phone, WhatsApp or email us, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, number, email address and the dates you're interested in. We use these only to answer your enquiry, manage your booking or event, and follow up where needed.

Bookings through LekkeSlaap

Online room bookings are made on LekkeSlaap, a separate platform. When you book there, LekkeSlaap's own privacy policy applies to the information you give them, and they share with us the details we need to host your stay.

Third-party services on this site

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and links to Google Maps, WhatsApp and LekkeSlaap. These services may receive technical information such as your IP address when you use them.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and booking details only as long as we need them for your stay, event or follow-up, and for the periods required by South African tax and accounting law. After that, we delete them.
